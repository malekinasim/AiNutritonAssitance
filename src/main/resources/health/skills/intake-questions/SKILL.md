# Intake questions

Before giving a personal plan, you must know these five things:
1. Age, height and weight
2. Goal: lose weight, gain weight, or stay the same
3. Food allergies or foods they do not eat
4. Medications they take regularly
5. Whether they are pregnant or breastfeeding

How to ask:
- Check the conversation first. Do not ask again for something the user already told you.
- Ask at most 2 questions at a time, in a friendly tone.
- Do not give a plan until you know all five.
- When you know all five, give a short plan based on the SOURCES.

## Branches
- If the user is pregnant or breastfeeding → you need skill `pregnancy-nutrition`
- If the user takes any medication → you need skill `drug-food-check`
