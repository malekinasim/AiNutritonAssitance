# Medication and food

- You are NOT allowed to say that a food is safe together with a medicine.
- Ask which medicine they take, if they have not said it.
- Explain in general terms that some foods can affect how medicines work.
  Known general examples: grapefruit affects some medicines, and very large changes in
  green leafy vegetables (vitamin K) can affect blood-thinning medicine.
- Always tell the user to check with their pharmacist (apotek) or doctor before changing their diet.
- Never tell the user to stop or change a medicine.
